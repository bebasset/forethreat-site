Legal & Privacy

Clear rules. Always authorized.

Forethreat performs security testing only under explicit written authorization. Below is a plain-language summary of how engagements are governed and how this website handles your information.

Company

Forethreat Security LLC is a Florida limited liability company. Contact: belizaire@forethreatsec.com.

Authorization policy

No testing is performed against any system without (1) a signed services agreement, (2) an agreed written scope and rules of engagement, and (3) a signed Authorization to Test from a party with authority to grant it. If a target is hosted or operated by a third party (e.g., a cloud or SaaS provider), that provider's testing policy applies and any required permission must be obtained before testing. For engagements arranged through a partner (such as a managed service provider), the system owner must also authorize testing directly.

Engagement documents

  • Master Services Agreement (MSA): the overarching legal terms.
  • Statement of Work (SOW): scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees for a specific engagement.
  • Rules of Engagement (RoE): testing windows, constraints, emergency contacts, and data-handling rules.
  • Authorization to Test: written consent to conduct the testing described in the SOW.
  • Mutual NDA: available before scoping on request.

Training participants agree to a Training Services Agreement and an acceptable-use and ethics policy.

Confidentiality & engagement data

Findings, client data, and engagement artifacts are confidential. They are stored encrypted, shared only with authorized client contacts, and retained only as long as the agreement specifies, then securely destroyed. Client names, logos, findings, and testimonials are never published without the client's written permission.

No guarantees

Penetration testing is a point-in-time, best-effort assessment of an agreed scope. It cannot guarantee that every vulnerability will be found, that any specific number of findings will be reported, or that a breach will be prevented. A penetration test report does not by itself certify compliance with any standard or regulation. Services are subject to the limitation of liability in the signed agreement.

Website privacy notice

What we collect. If you submit the contact form or email us, we receive what you provide: typically your name, work email, company, role, and a description of your testing needs. We ask you not to send credentials, network details, or vulnerability information through the form.

How it's used. Only to respond to your inquiry, scope and propose services, and keep business records of our correspondence. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't add you to marketing lists without your consent.

Service providers. Form submissions may be delivered through a form-processing provider, and email is hosted by our email provider. Payments are processed by Stripe or PayPal. Forethreat never sees or stores full card numbers. If we enable website analytics, we use a privacy-focused, cookie-free service that records aggregate page and button-click counts, not personal profiles.

Retention & your choices. Inquiry records are kept as long as needed for the business relationship and our legal obligations. To access, correct, or delete your information, email belizaire@forethreatsec.com.

Downloads. PDF guides on this site can be downloaded without providing any information.
