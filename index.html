FAQ

Questions buyers ask before booking

How much does a penetration test cost?

It depends on scope. Web application tests start at $4,500, API tests at $4,000, external network tests at $3,500, and internal/Active Directory tests at $6,500. You get a fixed price in writing after the scoping call. See pricing.

Will the report satisfy our SOC 2 auditor or customer?

Our reports are built for that use: scope, methodology, dates, findings, and retest status, plus an attestation letter on request. Whether a specific auditor or customer accepts a report is their decision, so tell us their requirements during scoping and we'll design the test to match.

Do you guarantee you'll find vulnerabilities?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. You're paying for thorough, documented testing of the agreed scope. If we find nothing significant, the report shows exactly what was tested and how.

Do you just run automated scanners?

No. Tools help with coverage, but the findings that matter, like broken access control, tenant isolation failures, and business logic flaws, require manual testing. Every reported issue is validated by hand.

How soon can you start?

Usually 2–4 weeks after the agreement is signed, depending on the schedule. Tell us your deadline on the scoping call and we'll say up front whether we can meet it.

Will testing break production?

We agree on testing windows, rate limits, and off-limits actions in the rules of engagement, and we can test a staging environment if you have one. Destructive actions are never performed without explicit approval.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. A mutual NDA is available before the scoping call if you need one, and confidentiality is part of every engagement agreement.

How do payment and retesting work?

A 50% deposit confirms your testing dates, and the balance is due when the report is delivered. You can pay by ACH, card, or PayPal. One retest of fixed findings within 60 days of the report is included.

Need a pentest report by a specific date?

Tell us what needs testing and when. You'll get a clear scope and fixed price, and a straight answer about whether we can meet your timeline.
