FAQ

Common questions

Do you require authorization?

Always. We only test with written authorization, an agreed scope, and signed rules of engagement.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. A mutual NDA is standard for every engagement.

Do you just run automated scanners?

No. Testing is manual and hands-on, so every finding is confirmed with real proof.

Do you offer retesting?

Yes. One retest is included with every one-off test, and retesting is built into retainers.

How do I pay?

Card, ACH, or PayPal. Installments and prepaid retainers are available. See Pricing.

How long does a test take?

Most engagements take 3–8 testing days, depending on scope. You'll get a timeline with your quote.
