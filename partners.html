01Two ways to work together

Choose who owns the client relationship

Referral partner

You introduce the client and we contract with them directly. You stay involved in remediation, which is often billable work for you.

  • 10% referral fee on the first engagement's collected fees
  • 5% on renewals and retests for 12 months
  • Paid within 15 days of our client's payment
  • You're copied on the readout if the client agrees

White-label / subcontract

You contract with your client and we deliver under your brand or co-branded. You set the client price.

  • Partner pricing at 15% below our published starting prices
  • Report on your template or co-branded
  • Your client still signs a direct authorization to test (non-negotiable)
  • Mutual NDA and non-solicitation of your clients
Referral fees and partner pricing are confirmed in a written partner agreement. Where a client's regulations or contracts require the tester to be disclosed, we disclose it.

How a partner engagement runs

  1. You share the client's need. We send a scoping questionnaire.
  2. We send a fixed-fee quote to you (white-label) or the client (referral).
  3. The client signs the authorization to test and rules of engagement.
  4. We test and report findings, and coordinate critical issues with you immediately.
  5. You remediate, then we retest and issue an updated report.

Ground rules that protect you

  • We never market to or solicit your clients directly
  • We don't sell managed IT services, so we don't compete with you
  • Findings are confidential to you and the client
  • We test only systems the client can authorize
