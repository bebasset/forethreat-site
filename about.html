About Forethreat Security

Founder-led penetration testing, done by hand.

Forethreat Security LLC is a penetration testing firm founded in 2026. It's deliberately small: the person you speak with on the scoping call is the person who tests your systems, writes your report, and verifies your fixes.

Founder & lead penetration tester

Belizaire Bassette II

Belizaire is a penetration tester specializing in web application, API, and network security, with particular depth in access control, authentication, and server-side vulnerabilities, the classes of issues behind most serious application breaches.

He has worked as a penetration tester at a PTaaS consultancy since December 2025 (starting as an intern), performing black-box and grey-box web application, API, and network assessments and writing CVSS-rated reports with reproduction steps and remediation guidance. That work was performed as an employee of another firm. Those clients are not Forethreat clients and are never named or referenced.

Before offensive security he worked in IT risk and compliance, including HIPAA-aligned access-control and policy audits in a healthcare environment. That background keeps remediation advice realistic for teams with limited time.

B.S. Information Management & Technology (Information Security concentration, Computer Science minor), Syracuse University. Competed for Syracuse in the NCAE Cyber Games regionals.

01How we work

Principles you can hold us to

Authorization first

No testing without a signed agreement, written scope, rules of engagement, and authorization from someone with the authority to give it.

Proof over noise

Every finding is validated by hand and backed by evidence. Scanner output is never pasted into a report.

Honest scoping

We recommend the smallest test that meets your actual requirement, and tell you when you don't need one.

No guaranteed findings

We don't promise a number of vulnerabilities. We promise thorough, documented testing of the agreed scope.

Confidentiality

Client names and findings are never disclosed without written permission. NDAs are standard.

Capacity limits

We take on only as many engagements as one senior tester can deliver well, and tell you the real start date up front.

Talk to the tester, not a salesperson

A 30-minute scoping call covers what you need tested, what it costs, and when it can be done.
