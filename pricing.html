No discount games

We don't inflate prices to offer discounts. Bundled scopes (e.g., web + API, or external + internal) are quoted as one engagement, so shared setup and reporting time is priced once.

Payments are only taken against a signed proposal or agreement. Deposits become non-refundable once testing begins. Full terms are in the engagement agreement (summary).
